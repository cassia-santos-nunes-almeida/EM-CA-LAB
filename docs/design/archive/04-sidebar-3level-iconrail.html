Faraday's Law
Experiment · Changing flux

Every electrical transformer relies on Faraday's law: a changing current in one coil creates a changing magnetic flux that induces a voltage in a neighbouring coil — the principle behind the entire power grid.

emf = −N · dΦ/dt   [ V = turns × Wb/s ]

Bring a magnet toward the coil and watch the induced current. But commit a prediction first — the bench stays dark until you do.
