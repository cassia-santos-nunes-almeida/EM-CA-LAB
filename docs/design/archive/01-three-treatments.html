Faraday's Law

Move a magnet through a coil and a voltage appears across its ends. The faster the flux through the loop changes, the larger the induced emf — and its sign always opposes the change.

emf = −N · dΦ/dt   [ V = turns × Wb/s ]

Before you run the bench, commit to a prediction. The instrument stays dark until you do — that's the whole point: a guess you've committed to is one you'll remember.
